# Coverage and richness

A profile preserves useful understanding of the person's current work, life, and concerns, with relevant earlier context. It is not one takeaway per source, a list of personality labels, or one sentence per module. Simplify the organization, not the information.

This reference primarily defines the quality of initial extraction. Explore the five modules before the first delivery, even when one source must support the baseline. Routine incremental runs assess only new information and affected context; they need not rediscover all facets or produce new content in every module. No useful changes, or a single useful addition, is a valid incremental result.

## Explore facets in the display order

These are discovery prompts, not required fields or conclusions to invent.

| Module | Facets to investigate |
| --- | --- |
| Background | Current work and life circumstances; resolved company/product/legal-employer relationships; role titles versus responsibility scope; products and responsibilities; meaningful professional relationships; clients and customers; parallel commitments; relevant non-work routines, learning, community activities, or personal projects; current priorities, choices, and transitions; concise relevant earlier experience |
| Communication Style | Recognizable tone and register, warmth, confidence, directness and pressure; what the person foregrounds and how that emphasis appears; greetings and opening moves; message progression; request type, intensity and variants; contextual pace and format; feedback and disagreement; sign-offs; actual contrasts across audiences/purposes. Distinguish personally authored voice from an attributable communication approach adopted for work. |
| Working Style | Connected working practices: inputs and constraints, rhythm and task grouping, decision criteria and quality standards, research and validation, responsibility boundaries, collaboration and handoffs, the function of tools or assistance, review, delivery, and follow-through. Preserve supported variations across parallel activities and changes over time. Adapt discovery to the person's actual work rather than imposing a standard workflow. |
| Preferences | Information order and depth by situation; formats and deliverables; review inputs; collaboration arrangements; interaction approaches; explicitly stated tools/channels; recurring corrections; relevant personal interests and practical preferences supported by clear evidence |
| Writing Style | Sentence rhythm; paragraph construction; vocabulary; punctuation; formatting; examples and rhetorical devices; language use; differences by genre. Add expression mechanics beyond the complete communicative account; avoid duplicating that account here. |

Background should organize this context by company: the main current company first, other active parallel companies/engagements next, and concise earlier experience afterward. Within each company, connect business context, the subject's role, products/projects, collaborators/customers, and meaningful progress. Read [Company-centered current work](company-context.md) for concurrent roles and lightweight public enrichment. Do not split the same company across separate global lists of products, people, and milestones.

For non-company contexts, preserve the same relationship-first reasoning using the actual institution, independent practice, or activity. Do not search for a missing corporate role, product, or customer segment when it is irrelevant. The facet list guides discovery; it does not prescribe what kind of person the profile must describe.

Background can contain substantial professional context. A relevant company, product, collaborator, customer segment, or milestone is not disposable detail merely because it is specific or evolving. Explain relationships: what a product is, the user's connection to it, what they own, whom they work with, and which customers or clients it serves, where supported. Use topical grouping when it helps reading; retain the five top-level modules.

For Working Style, read [Concrete working practices](working-style.md). Look beyond what the person is responsible for to how the work is performed, and retain useful operational detail in the connected account. Software lists, recurring keywords, and general trait labels do not replace an explanation of the method.

## Expand before synthesizing

1. Inspect diverse relevant authorized information regardless of author, including all participants' contributions, shared records, and useful system-generated material. Keep raw material transient; do not create an event ledger or mail archive.
2. Build a transient inventory of distinct useful observations. Classify each as an explicit statement, documented fact or action, attributed report, observed pattern, user-confirmed understanding, or unsupported content. Connect complementary observations across sources to reconstruct the subject's role, activities, relationships, and circumstances before condensing them.
3. Scope attribution checks to the claim after reading. Others' correspondence or records may directly document the subject's behavior, choices, constraints, and scoped preferences as well as professional context. Evidence must concern the subject but need not be authored by the subject. Personal wording requires attributable prose or edits; keep this narrow requirement from becoming a filter on the overall corpus.
4. Separate independence from overlap. A role, a product responsibility, a client relationship, and a decision criterion are not duplicates because they occur in the same thread. Several observations may share one general source association.
5. Merge equivalent meanings. Combine complementary detail only when the combined text preserves all of it intelligibly. Retain distinct conditions, people, projects, periods, and exceptions when useful.
6. Compose connected topical passages, then write a developed opening portrait of present work, life, and concerns, with selective history. Follow [Narrative and communication style](narrative-and-style.md) without imposing its discovery dimensions as a prose template. Compare the modules and overview with the inventory and restore valuable detail lost during composition.

## Depth follows the evidence

There is no target number, point cap, word cap, fixed sentence length, or requirement for equal module sizes. Retain every distinct, relevant, supported observation. Sparse material may support few observations; rich correspondence may justify a long profile, especially detailed professional background. Do not add filler to make a module appear complete.

Use topical subheadings and connected explanatory paragraphs with enough context to remain useful. Reserve numbered lists for actual sequences and bullets for comparable variants. Clarity comes from grouping related information, removing true repetition, and making relationships explicit. Do not collapse rich facts into generic descriptions such as works in technology or communicates efficiently. The overview should make the person understandable on its own while the modules retain the full useful detail.

During first extraction, explore relevant work and non-work content within authorization before deciding what belongs in the opening portrait. A work-heavy inbox must not automatically crowd out a well-supported current personal project or meaningful life commitment. Conversely, a subscription, invitation, travel receipt, or recommendation alone does not establish a lasting interest, attendance, residence, or lifestyle. Keep supported circumstances at their actual scope and do not expand private access merely to balance the overview.

## Diagnose a sparse initial mailbox result

Before finishing with only one observation, inspect the cause:

- Did reading stop at search snippets or the first recent page?
- Were received, sent, archived, and relevant older messages considered within the authorized scope?
- Did a Sent-only search discard context about companies, products, people, customers, client work, or career changes?
- Were names collected without resolving which were companies, products, legal employers, clients, tools, or personas?
- Was an explicit role title replaced by an occupation guessed from frequent email activities?
- Did template/style filtering throw away useful campaign, segment, review-routing, automation, or tool context?
- Did the profile over-expand resume history and easy administrative exchanges while leaving the current work ecosystem underexplored?
- Were automation, HR, recruiting, receipts, or notifications wrongly excluded as categories instead of assessed for relevance?
- Were searches diversified across identity, responsibilities, products, partnerships, decisions, reviews, feedback, and milestones in the actual source languages?
- Were documented facts rejected because they were not self-statements or lifelong traits?
- Were supported behavior patterns rejected because the user had not explicitly named a preference?
- Were multiple facts from one source incorrectly compressed into one block?
- Did concision erase detail or did a raw quotation requirement prevent retention of a derived understanding?
- Did the final text merely list disconnected observations?
- Did communication style stop at adjectives, describe tasks instead of tone, or give generic drafting advice instead of identifying observable habits and emphasis?
- Did easy administrative replies or the current chat displace substantive communication in other available contexts?
- Did template filtering erase a communication approach the subject demonstrably used, or did template volume get mistaken for independent evidence of natural voice?

Correct the actual failure. If the available material truly supports only one observation, retain it and describe the actual limit. Do not invent traits or demand another connection before delivering existing value.

## Complete the initial profile based on coverage

During initial extraction, track promising facets transiently as supported, examined but unsupported, irrelevant, or inaccessible. Revisit relevant gaps. Stop when varied reads cease adding meaningful understanding, the authorized scope is exhausted, or a genuine access/resource limit is reached. State partial scope honestly; never claim a complete mailbox review after sampling a small subset. Routine incremental reading instead ends when the eligible new-information interval has been assessed, even if it adds no profile content.

Only advertising and spam are categorical exclusions. Relevance, attribution, and evidential strength determine whether other individual messages contribute. A transactional message may establish a useful product relationship or milestone without justifying storage of an invoice number or a full purchase history.

## Fictional retention example

A mailbox might support all of the following as distinct understanding:

- The person works at an organization with a particular professional remit.
- They own a named product's onboarding and collaborate with a design lead.
- A second product serves a different customer segment and has a different project status.
- They coordinate an external client engagement alongside internal product work.
- Incoming operational correspondence documents a meaningful delivery milestone.
- Recruitment correspondence shows a career opportunity under discussion; it does not establish acceptance.
- Their own planning messages clarify the user problem before selecting a solution.
- Their reviews focus on observable changes and concrete next actions.
- They use brief internal updates but more background in customer explanations.
- They explicitly prefer decisions and next steps near the start of recurring updates.
- Their authored emails use short paragraphs and structured requests.

Preserve the supported relationships, roles, distinctions, and context, not just the labels above. This is an internal coverage illustration, not the intended flat-list output layout, an expected count, or verified information about an actual user. Provide one general mailbox source overview without retaining underlying messages.
