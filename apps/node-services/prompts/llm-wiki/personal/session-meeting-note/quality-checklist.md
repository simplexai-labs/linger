# Final Self-Check

## Content

- The original source, material scope, and time information are grounded; nothing is invented.
- The transcript has been checked segment by segment; the opening, later segments, and independent secondary information are all attributed.
- The summary and body are organized as "topic → sub-topic → point → explanation"; the same question is merged and different information is kept.
- Every valid piece of information has specific written attribution; "already covered" or text inside an image does not replace the body.
- No added causation, priority, judgment, or consensus; people, numbers, conditions, and undecided status are accurate.
- To-dos have an action basis in the source; when there are no explicit to-dos, say none were identified.

## Format

- Headings do not skip levels; long content has been split into short paragraphs, sibling items, or real sub-items.
- Each item carries one main idea; card points can be read on their own and are not keyword piles.
- Bold boundaries, punctuation, blank lines, and list indentation are correct; no asterisks, code fences, or image syntax are exposed.

## Images

- Compare item by item with the reference screenshot: multi-color light outer shell, white inner panel, line icons and titles in the same color, dot-separated items, bold keywords. Fix anything missing; successful file generation or loading does not replace visual acceptance.

- The summary image matches the user's screenshot: light outer shell, white content panel, same-color title and the specified line icon; the main title, core conclusion, major topics, and card levels are clear.
- Cards are logical siblings arranged horizontally in discussion order; no mixing of parents and children, no arrows or connectors; equal height within a row, adaptive across rows; each point on its own line with bold keywords, and no truncation, overlap, or tiny text.
- Local flowcharts or mind maps are used only when the source has explicit relationships, and the text is still kept in full.
- Image paths are real, relative, and deliverable with the Markdown; no Base64 or fake links.

## Verification

Distinguish "file generated", "PNG viewed", and "product preview checked". Mark truthfully any check that was not actually done.
