# Topic Discussion Body

Write the output in the user's language (the language the meeting or conversation was held in).

After the summary, expand the specific discussion. The body is more detailed than the summary image but does not restate every sentence.

## Levels

```markdown
## Main topic [HH:MM:SS - HH:MM:SS]

Start with one paragraph describing the scope and necessary background.

### Sub-topic

- **Specific discussion point**: describe one object or judgment.
  - **Reason/condition**: the basis, limit, or premise the source explicitly mentions.
  - **Change/result**: later corrections, disagreements, or how far the discussion got.
```

- Organize main topics by content relationships and keep only one representative time range; when it is missing, write "time to be confirmed".
- Sibling headings have a similar level of abstraction; parent and child must have real containment. Do not create subordination only because of shared keywords or adjacent times.
- Expand each object that had substantive discussion separately. Content that was only mentioned may be merged into a brief note, but do not add information the source does not have.
- Prioritize background, reasons, approaches, limits, examples, disagreements, corrections, and results; merge repeated persuasion.
- Specific discussion points use a short title plus an explanation; one item must not carry several independent topics. Split long explanations into sub-items or short paragraphs.

## Reading Standard

Someone who did not attend should understand: who or what was discussed, why, which options were discussed, which limits exist, and how far the discussion got. Background the source does not explain is written as "not stated in the meeting"; do not infer it. When attribution is unclear, use "someone proposed" and do not guess the speaker.

The topic list in the body stays consistent with the summary image. The summary image uses major-topic text groupings and sectioned cards as needed, while the body keeps the full hierarchy; do not add a "module details" wrapper layer.
