# Open Questions

Write the output in the user's language (the language the meeting or conversation was held in).

When the meeting really left items unconfirmed, list them separately after the topics, briefly stating what is still undecided and, when needed, which topic it belongs to. If there are no unconfirmed items, omit the whole section; do not write "none" or an empty heading. Extracting open questions does not mean raising more questions of your own.
