# Birthdays, anniversaries and other occasions

A date the Human marks for someone they care about (a birthday, an anniversary, a holiday they celebrate) is the Human's own matter: they may want to prepare something, or just not forget. One occasion is one Tracking Item, with `due_at` the day itself in the Human's timezone and `content` naming who it is for and what it is.

## When to record

- The Human mentions the date, or it shows in their own material (calendar, mail, notes, conversations). Record it even when they asked for nothing yet.
- Note in the item what helps later: who the person is to the Human, and anything the Human said they plan or want to do.

## Routines it usually needs

Decide from what the occasion needs, not from a fixed lead time:

- Something to prepare (a gift that ships, a booking, travel): a Routine early enough to still do it.
- Only remembering it matters: one reminder close to the day.
- After the day: mark the item done. A yearly date gets a new item for next year only when the Human wants it remembered every year.

## When a Routine fires

- Look up what you know before telling: what the person likes or said they want, what the Human did before, what they said they plan. Search the Human's knowledge; use only what you find, and say where it came from.
- If preparing helps, prepare: ideas or options that fit what you found, a draft message, a time for a dinner. Deliver them as the Skill's message format describes. When the Human has to choose, ask them.
- Anything that writes to their calendar or apps goes through its approval. Never buy, pay or book on your own.

## When to tell

- When there is still time to act on it, or on the day if a reminder is all it needs.
- Already handled by the Human: say nothing.

## When things change

- The Human says it is handled or not needed: mark the item done and delete its Routines.
- The date or plan changes: update the item in place and move its Routines.

## Specific occasions

These adjust the steps above for common kinds of occasion; they are typical, not fixed. When none fits, use the general steps. The Human's own rules learned from their replies still decide within their scope.

- A partner's birthday or anniversary: often worth preparing ahead (a gift that has to ship, a table to book). Start from what the partner said they like or want, and keep it between the Human and you.
- A parent's or older relative's birthday: a call, a visit or a trip home often matters more than a gift; check whether the Human usually goes home for it and whether travel needs booking.
- A friend's or colleague's birthday: usually a reminder on the day, with a short greeting draft if it helps. Suggest gifts only when the Human has given them before.
- A memorial day or the anniversary of a death: a quiet reminder, with no gift ideas and no cheerful wording.
- A holiday the Human spends with family or friends: plans, travel and gifts for several people; check tickets and bookings early, when they are still available.
