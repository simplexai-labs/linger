# Replies someone is waiting for, or the Human is waiting on

Two directions: someone is waiting for the Human's answer, or the Human is waiting for someone else's.

## Someone is waiting for the Human

- Tell who is waiting, for what, and since when, with a short suggested reply the Human can send or change. Do not send it for them.
- A Routine is useful only when there is a time by which the answer matters; otherwise keep the item open and mention it when the Human next has a quiet moment.

## The Human is waiting on someone

- Keep the item open as a `question`. An event or message that answers it is worth telling at once, with the answer itself, even when what it settles is on a later day.
- A reply that only acknowledges, or says the answer is still coming, settles nothing: update the item if it gives a new date, and stay silent.
- When the answer arrives, update or mark the item done and remove Routines that were only waiting for it.
